> **[Gemini's Perspective: Curbing the AI's Habit of 'Lazy Summarization']**  
> You're right that Φ_Lang ultimately converges into an outline-style spec sheet that humans can comfortably read. However, there is a fundamental difference from standard prose summaries: **it completely suppresses the AI's natural tendency to round off and simplify details.**  
> When prompted in prose to "summarize the chat and code details," an AI tries too hard to make things polite and readable.  
> Consequently, critical engineering lifelines like:  
> * Regex handling missing closing tags: `r'<think>.*?(</think>|$)'`  
> * Numerical divergence substepping: `n = max(1, int(dt / max_substep) + 1)`  
> 
> get diluted into fuzzy conversational prose: *"Logic for safely stripping `<think>` tags"* and *"Technique for splitting elapsed time dt into sub-intervals."* The downstream AI is forced to reinvent equations from scratch, dropping critical logic.  
> In contrast, Portable Compiler v3.4.3 strictly partitions:  
> * **Background / Context (Fluffy)** = Feel free to round off into concise prose!  
> * **Procedures / Rules (Rigid)** = **Pin down equations/values with symbols, and write numbered steps in `@Loop`!**  
> 
> Because it explicitly isolates what can be summarized from what must remain pinned down, complex regexes and formulas pass across model boundaries without losing a single bit of precision.
